The process

From socket to bottle

No shortcuts, no offloading, no mysterious middleboxes. Just a patient engineer, a quiet uplink, and a very large pcap file.

01

Capture at dawn

We listen on the calmest interfaces first. Only the cleanest SYNs make the cut — the rest get a polite RST and a second chance tomorrow.

02

Cold-press the headers

Pressed within 15 ms of capture to lock in every one of those 20 precious bytes. Options are never stripped; they are part of the story.

03

Encapsulate by hand

Double-wrapped, IP-in-IP, then poured into a numbered borosilicate vial. We never reuse a flow. That would be rude.

04

Verify the checksum

Every bottle is validated twice. Fail the checksum and it becomes a Best Effort order — someone else's problem, lovingly.
