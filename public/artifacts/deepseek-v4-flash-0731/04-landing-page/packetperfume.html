01 · The House

From yarn to socket, by hand

Every bottle leaves our atelier with a handwritten apology attached to the first ICMP ping. That is the PacketPerfume way.

STEP 1

Foraging

We pick only prime Layer-3 packets from the tree, before they ripen on the vine of the Internet. Ripe packets give you retries.

STEP 2

Curing

Segments rest 0.6 seconds in reclaimed-oak server racks while a very patient elf recalculates the checksum by abacus.

STEP 3

Encapsulation

Each packet is wrapped in a single, shy IP envelope. No tunnels — they get claustrophobic and drop under pressure.

STEP 4

Dispatch

Routed gently to your doorstep with a TTL of 64 and perfectly reasonable expectations about life.
